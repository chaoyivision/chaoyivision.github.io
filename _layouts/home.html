About Me


I conduct scaling laws research in Bytedance Seed Foundation Team (Seed-[1.5](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seed1_5_vl)/[1.6](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seed1_6)/[1.8](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/official-release-of-seed1-8-a-generalized-agentic-model)/[2.0](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/seed-2-0-official-launch)), supporting the scaling, interpretability, and debugging of foundation LLMs/VLMs. My research on scaling laws focuses on three directions: (1) improving their predictability, (2) expanding their coverage, and (3) relaxing their constraints — always with an optimistic mindset!

Previously, I completed my PhD in 2023, during which I spent joyful and quality time at Meta FAIR, Amazon AWS AI Lab, Microsoft GenAI and Google Cloud AI - super grateful to all my mentors and peers. My PhD topics were Medical Computer Vision and 3D Scene Understanding, fortunately supervised by A/Prof Weidong Cai and A/Prof Yang Song. Before that, I also got my bachelor degree from the University of Sydney in Australia, with a university medal received in computer science.
